This is the size of the occupation, not a count of people performing the benchmark. [O*NET](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/49-9071.00), [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/emp/skills/mechanical.htm)

Our separate reference block, fixed offset, remote display and button-actuated platform form an isolated test fixture. They are not a replica of that manufacturer's procedure or certified hardware. [Manufacturer manual, sections 7.1–7.2](https://www.stabila.com/files/default/pdf/en/mptech196dl_mptech196mdl_manual_en.pdf)

The task combines physical instrument relocation, reference-based interpretation and measured adjustment. Ordinary pick-and-place does not by itself satisfy the scorer. Wrong/cancelling presses and excessive presses reduce the score. The existing native demo establishes feasibility for the specified layout. The fixed offset and layout leave generalisation untested.

The guide requires novelty, real-world relevance and physical validity in addition to feasibility. The frequency of this exact subtask remains unquantified. [Guide](https://tasks.robocurve.org/#rubric)

Residual review risk: organisers may regard the fixture as too narrow or simplified to represent enough occupational work. The evidence supports a bounded maintenance subskill, not a claim that 1.62 million jobs are automated.
